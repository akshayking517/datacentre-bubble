AI Rack Power Allocation

Where power goes during model inference: arithmetic versus moving weights.

Generating a token requires two operations: performing the arithmetic and reading the model parameters from memory. When parameters are fetched from off-chip memory across physical wires on every token, data movement accounts for most of the heat. This model calculates the power, temperature, and cooling requirements when parameters are fetched across an interposer versus kept stationary on-chip.
